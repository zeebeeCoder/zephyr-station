# Zephyr enclosure system: design brief for the Fusion 360 spec

Date 26.10.2026. Reader: Astra, the owner's Fusion 360 agent. Scope: the electronics and battery box, the BME680 radiation shield, the PMS7003 housing and the mast mounts. The box is for the new custom PCB only.

**Labels.** Every number carries one of these labels:
- **ds** is a datasheet, standard or maker's guide.
- **meas** is a third-party measurement.
- **calc** is computed here or by the earlier model.
- **assumed** is engineering judgement.
- **(secondary)** means the finding behind the number was not read at its source.
- **[n]** points to Section 8.

---

## 1. Summary

1. **Main box.** One sealed, wall-hung box in white ASA. It is 242 × 162 × 76 mm outside and 210 × 130 × 70 mm inside. The lid is the front face and faces NE.
2. **Box contents.** The PCB sits at the NW end. A foam-lined battery compartment is built into the box at the SE end, at the bottom.
3. **Lid seal.** A bought 3.0 mm EPDM cord sits in a 2.2 × 4.0 mm face groove. The lid closes on a hard stop with 14 M3 screws into brass inserts.
4. **Entries.** All entries go through the floor: 5 Lapp M12 glands and 1 SMA bulkhead. A Gore M12 vent sits low on the NW end wall. There is no drain hole. A 10 g silica-gel sachet clips to the lid.
5. **Wi-Fi antenna.** The ESP32 PCB antenna sits at the top right. A 15 mm keep-out in every direction is modelled as a body. No metal, cell, cable or carbon-filled part may enter it.
6. **Roof cap.** A white ASA cap stands 20 mm above the box top and is open on all sides. It falls 10° toward the lid and overhangs it by 25 mm.
7. **PMS7003 hood.** An open-bottom white ASA hood screws under the box floor at the SE end. The inlet faces down through a stainless mesh. The outlet is ducted sideways to the SE.
8. **Radiation shield.** 7 white ASA louvre plates (Ø120, pitch 20, 40° cone), a Ø130 roof and a solid bottom cap sit on 3 M4 A2 rods. The stack stands on a white ASA arm 160 mm out on the N side of the mast. The sensor is at about 1.60 m.
9. **Mast.** The box bracket and the shield arm share one 90° V-saddle with stainless hose clamps (25–60 mm masts). The box sits on the NE side, behind the SW-facing panel. The anemometer goes at the mast top.
10. **Materials.** White ASA for every outside part and for the seal path. PETG-CF only for shaded inner parts at least 15 mm from the antenna, and only with a hardened nozzle. TPU only for pads. No PLA in the field. A2 stainless hardware.
11. **Heat.** White plastic, the roof cap and 10 mm of foam (no thicker) keep the pack's modelled summer peak at 41 °C or less. If the logged pack temperature passes 38 °C, a side shade for the SE wall is added.
12. **Printing.** Every part fits a 256 mm bed in one piece, and none needs supports.

## 2. Design rules per module

### 2.0 Frame, global rules, Fusion parameters

**Box frame**
- Units are mm.
- Origin: the inner bottom-left-back corner of the box, seen from the lid.
- **X** runs left to right, from the SE end to the NW end.
- **Y** runs up.
- **Z** runs from the inner face of the back wall toward the lid. The lid's inner face is at Z = 70.
- Mast directions are azimuths in degrees from north.

**Global rules**
- **Holes:**
  - Model every functional hole 0.1 mm over nominal (meas [30]). Leave Bambu "X-Y hole compensation" at 0; never use both corrections (secondary [48][49]).
  - M3 clearance is 3.4 mm and M4 clearance is 4.6 mm (assumed, secondary).
- **Fits between printed parts:**
  - 0.25 mm per side in XY and at least 0.4 mm in Z (ds [47]).
  - Print mating parts in the same filament and the same orientation (ds [47]).
- **Cold:**
  - No snap-fits for structure or sealing; use screws only.
  - Inner corners of load-bearing parts get R ≥ 1.0. No latch or cantilever under 2 mm thick (assumed).
  - Reason: ASA notched impact drops from 17 to 4.0 kJ/m² between 23 °C and −30 °C (ds [44]).
- **Loads:**
  - Keep load paths in the XY plane.
  - Bosses that stand up in Z get 1.5 mm root fillets and gussets.
  - Printed ASA is 43.8 MPa in XY and 32 MPa in Z (ds [35]). Upright samples lost up to 18 % strength in a year outdoors, flat ones up to 4 % (meas [45]). The fillet size is assumed.
- **Surfaces:** keep outer faces smooth. No text, logos or grooves on upper faces (meas [80], insects; secondary).
- **Interference check:** model these as bodies and check them against everything:
  - the antenna keep-out (§ 2.3);
  - the PCB envelope;
  - the gland wrench circles (Ø24 for M12, Ø28 for M16).

**Printing the box and lid**
- Print the box back-wall down, opening up. The land is then a top surface; iron it (meas [13]).
- Print the lid outer face down and iron its sealing face.
- Place every Z-seam (aligned or painted) on the outer vertical edge at X = 0, Y = 130. Never put a seam in the groove or at an entry (meas [13]; ds [12]).
- Put no labels, nubs or other features on the land or on the rim perimeter (ds [12]).

**Drip edge**
- The lid overhangs the land by 6 mm on every side, so it needs no skirt (assumed).
- The roof's front overhang shields the top seam.

**Mounting tabs**
- 4 tabs in the back-wall plane, on the outside of the two end walls.
- Size 14 (X) × 20 (Y) × 11 (Z), centres at X = −9 and 219, Y = 10 and 120.
- Ø4.6 through-hole along Z; gusset each tab to its end wall.
- The tabs lie on the bed when the box prints.
- Use M4 through-bolts and nuts, not inserts. Through-bolted joints use nuts (ds [29][31]).
- The tabs are at least 40 mm from the antenna (calc).

**Outer ribs**
- These ribs stand vertical in print, so they need no supports. They are 10 wide and 8 proud of the wall, and run from Z = −2.4 to 45.
- **Top wall:** ribs at X = 12 and 200 carry the roof. M3 inserts at Z = 12 and 40.
- **Floor:** ribs at X = 8 and 97 carry the PMS hood. M3 inserts at Z = 22.
- All insert holes are blind. No screw passes through a sealed wall.

### 2.2 (b) Battery compartment and thermal strategy

**Geometry**
- **Compartment:** built into the box, inside X 0–100, Y 0–88, Z 0–62, i.e. 100 × 88 × 62 [P1 D11].
  - Its walls are the box's left wall, floor and back wall, plus a 2 mm divider (X 100–102, Y 0–90) and a 2 mm shelf (Y 88–90, X 0–102). Both print as vertical walls.
  - A lead slot of 20 × 6 runs through the shelf at X 80–100.
- **Pack:** a welded 2 × 4 block, about 80 × 42 × 68 and ≤ 0.40 kg [P1 D13].
  - Cell axes run **vertical** (the 68 mm along Y). The 88 mm height fits 68 + 2 × 10 foam only this way (calc).
- **Foam:** 10 mm closed-cell XPS on all six faces, or closed-cell PE. XPS is λ ≈ 0.033 W/m·K with 0.7 vol % water uptake (ds [11]).
  - A 3 mm slot takes the NTC lead (taped to the outermost cell) and the DS18B20.
- **Front closure:** a 2 mm printed retainer plate at Z 62–64 over X 0–100, Y 0–88, with the front foam panel behind it.
  - 2 × M3 screws go into inserts in bosses on the divider's front edge and on the left wall at Y 44.
- **Above the shelf** (Y 90–130, X 0–102, Z 0–60): the inline 5 A fuse holder and the 1S protection board, outside the foam [P1 D13] (secondary).
- The pack is at least 60 mm from the antenna (calc). B3 asked for 40 mm (assumed).

**Thermal levers**

**Limits**
- Hardware charge window: about +1.7 to +42.6 °C [P1 D5].
- Cells charge at 0–45 °C and discharge at −10 to +60 °C [P1 D13].

**Monitoring.** The DS18B20 on the pack logs the first summer and the first winter [P1 D13]. If the pack passes 38 °C, add the optional SE side shade (assumed trigger: 3.5 K under the 41.5 °C trip).

**Roof cap**
- **Plate:** 239 (X) × 103 (Z), 3.0 mm thick with two underside ribs.
- **Coverage:** X −14 to 225 (12 mm past each end wall) and Z −5 to 98 (25 mm past the lid face).
  - The overhang was cut from the 30 mm in B12 so the cap fits a 256 mm bed (calc).
- **Fall:** 10° toward the lid (B12, assumed).
- **Legs:** Ø10. The front pair (at Z 40) is 20 mm tall and the back pair (at Z 12) 25 mm, which gives the 10° fall (calc).
- **Air gap:** at least 20 mm everywhere and open on all four sides.
  - M2 asked for ≥ 15 mm (assumed). 20 mm also clears the 13.6 mm screw ears (calc).
- **Fixing:** 4 × M3 × 35 A2 screws with washers, from the top into the top-rib inserts. They are at least 31 mm from the antenna (calc).
- **Optional SE side shade:** white ASA, 2.0 mm, standing 15 mm off the SE end wall, open at top and bottom. It touches the box only through 3–4 Ø8 standoffs (M2, assumed, secondary).

**Gland and bulkhead rules**
- **Spacing:** glands at least 25 mm apart (centre to centre) and at least 15 mm from walls (assumed). The SMA is at least 30 mm from the glands and 60 mm from the ESP32 antenna (assumed, secondary). The layout above meets all of these (calc); the SMA is about 100 mm from the antenna.
- **Lapp ST-M glands** (ds [53]):
  - M12 clamps 3.5–7 mm, wrench 15. M16 clamps 4.5–10 mm, wrench 19. Both have an 8 mm thread.
  - Use the black version: it is the UV-rated one. Rated IP68/IP69, −40 to +100 °C.
  - Lock each with a SKINDICHT SM-M nut inside: wrench 15/19, 2.8–4.7 mm thick (ds [54], secondary).
  - Lapp's recommended hole size was not found. 12.3/16.3 mm is an estimate; confirm it on the test coupon.
- **Layer lines:** the floor is a vertical wall in print, so its outer face carries layer lines. Put a flat sealing ring under each gland outside: Lapp's matching ring if one exists, else a 1 mm EPDM washer (assumed). The hose test (§ 7) checks it.
- **SMA bulkhead:**
  - Panel cutout Ø6.5 ± 0.05 with a 5.85 flat (ds [58]).
  - Maximum panel thickness is 2.8–4.4 mm depending on the part (ds [58][59][60], secondary), so the 2.4 mm floor needs no thinning.
  - Pigtail: u.FL to SMA(f), 1.37 mm cable, 150–250 mm (ds [61]). Route it along the floor and never above Y = 60, which keeps it out of the antenna keep-out (assumed).

**ESP32 antenna**
- **Keep-out body:** 15 mm around the module's antenna in every direction (ds [1]). That is about X 150–198, Y 93–130, Z 0–35. Run an interference check against everything.
- **Distances (calc):**
  - the PCB's top-right hole and its brass insert: ≥ 20 mm;
  - the top inner wall: 15 mm;
  - the back wall: 16.6 mm;
  - the lid: about 50 mm;
  - the cells: ≥ 60 mm;
  - roof screws: ≥ 31 mm;
  - the mast: about 45 mm, if the mast is straight behind the box.
- **Wall near the antenna:** solid white ASA, 2.4 mm, with no infill, no metal and no carbon-filled filament.
  - The AISLER guide saw shifts of more than 20–30 MHz with under 3 mm clearance (meas [2]).
  - Espressif's 15 mm of air makes the wall thickness matter much less.
- **RF loss** (calc from permittivity data, meas [3]):
  - Through 2 mm of solid ASA: about 0.05 dB at 2.45 GHz and 0.01 dB at 868 MHz.
  - The 868 MHz antenna is outside, so the box material does not matter for LoRa. The gateway is 20 m away.

**USB-C.** There is no outside port. Plug in with the lid off (decision 6.2). A vertical receptacle uses the ≥ 33 mm of space in front of the PCB.

**Vent**
- Part: Gore PolyVent Standard M12x1.5, black (PMF100320), with counter nut M10510-009 (ds [55]):
  - airflow 450 ml/min at 70 mbar;
  - through-hole 12.2 ± 0.1 mm, chamfered;
  - flat area at least 20 mm across;
  - torque 0.7 ± 0.1 Nm;
  - tested −40 to +125 °C.
- Mount it on a vertical face with the cap outside (ds [55]): the NW end wall at Y 25, Z 35 (assumed).
- The roof cannot cover it. Snow or rime may cover it for a while (assumed harmless).

**Drainage**
- The sealed box has no drain or weep hole (B9).
- All entries face down.
- Each cable either rises to its gland from below, or has a drip loop 100–150 mm across with its low point at least 50 mm below the gland (ds [92]).

**Condensation**
- A sealed polymer box saturates with moisture within weeks (meas [56]). The vent stops pressure cycling from pumping in moist air (ds [55]).
- A 10 g indicating silica-gel sachet sits in a clip on the lid's inner face at X 10–60, Y 95–125. Replace it each autumn (assumed).
- The PCB is conformal-coated [P1 R-E4].

**Electronics in the shield**
- Only the breakout goes inside: no I²C extender, regulator board or connector PCB. An extender inside a printed shield read about 1.5 °C high (meas [72]).
- Remove or black out the power LED.

**Coating and glue near the sensor** (ds [82])
- Keep the breakout out of the conformal coating, or mask the BME680 and its 0.35 mm vent with silicone-free tape.
- Use only siloxane-free coating.
- No hot glue, silicone or rubber grommets inside the shield.

**No fan**
- Leave out the fan and the fan seat. The winter energy budget has no room for one [P1 D13], and aspiration wets the sensor in fog (ds [6]).
- Aspiration would cut calm-sun errors from about 2 °C to about 0.5 °C (ds [90]). A later fan roof (40 × 40, blowing out the top) stays possible (secondary [72]).

**Insects**
- No mesh on the louvre gaps.
- If spiders become a problem, put fine mesh over the cap drains only (secondary [87]).

**Maintenance**
- Emboss the plate index only on the inside face of a boss.
- Check monthly and wash with soap and water; no solvents (secondary [81][88]).

**Cat5e**
- PE jacket, 5.2 mm, rated −20 to +70 °C in use and 0 to +50 °C for installation (ds [86]). Install it above 0 °C.

**BME680 duty cycle**
- Forced mode once per 15 min, with power on for under 1 s per reading. No BSEC LP mode (ds [93]; secondary [95]).

**Accuracy to state in the docs**
- 0.4 / 0.7 / 1.5 °C RMS at 3 / 2 / 1 m/s wind with about 1000 W/m² of sun (ds [73]).
- About +2 °C at noon in calm sun (ds [96]).
- RMSE 1.22 °C for a printed passive shield (meas [80]).
- Up to about 10 °C over fresh snow at 1 m/s (meas [83]).

### 2.6 (f) Mast mounting and layout on the mast

**Box bracket** (white ASA, not PETG-CF; § 5)
- **Shape:** a 6 mm plate, 240 × 130. Two bars, 20 mm tall, at box Y 10 and 120, joined by a 60 mm-wide spine.
- **Spacers:** 4 Ø12 × 10 bosses give a 10 mm air gap to the box (secondary).
- **Box bolts:** 4 × M4 × 35 A2 through-bolts with DIN 9021 washers on both sides and nylon-insert nuts, snug at about 1 Nm (assumed).

## 3. Materials and print settings per part (enclosed Bambu, 0.4 mm nozzle)

**Common ASA profile.** Start from the Bambu Studio system profile for your ASA brand, then:
- **Temperatures:** nozzle 260 °C (ds [36]: 260 ± 10). Bed 100 °C on textured PEI; the sources span 75–110 °C, so 100 °C is assumed (ds [35][36]; ds [76]).
- **Cooling:** part fan 0–20 %, up to 50 % on bridges and overhangs only (ds [36]).
- **Chamber:** door and top closed. Pre-warm the chamber for 10 min with the bed at 100 °C (assumed).
- **Filament:** dry at 70 °C for 7 h (ds [35]).
- **Brim:** 3 mm on parts longer than 150 mm, 5 mm on the round shield plates (ds [36]; ds [76]).
- **Room:** ventilate it, because ASA releases styrene (ds [37]).

**Watertight additions** for the box shell and lid (ds [12]; meas [13][14]; top/bottom count assumed):
- 6 wall loops at about 0.42 mm, so the 2.4 mm walls are pure perimeters with no sparse infill.
- Flow ratio +5 % over the calibrated value (or 0.44 mm line width). Nozzle 265–270 °C.
- Layers 0.16–0.20 mm. 6 top and 6 bottom layers.
- 100 % infill in the thick zones (land, ears, ribs).
- If the water test still leaks, raise the infill/wall overlap to 25–35 %.

**Calibration before the real parts**
- **Shrinkage:** print a 150 mm L-bar flat in the same white ASA and measure it after 24 h. Set the filament "Shrinkage (XY)" to measured / designed × 100. Expect about 99.3–99.6 % (secondary [50]; ds [44] gives 0.40–0.70 % moulding shrinkage). Do not scale the CAD.
- **Holes:** model them +0.1 mm and leave "X-Y hole compensation" at 0 (meas [30]).

**Heat limits** for choosing a material:
- ASA: HDT at 0.45 MPa is 93–103 °C (ds [35][36]).
- PETG: HDT 68–78 °C (ds [38][39]).
- PETG-CF: HDT about 74 °C (secondary [43]).
- Sunlit dark surfaces reach 79–88 °C (meas [5]).
- So PETG and PETG-CF are used only below about 55 °C, inside shade (assumed).

**PETG-CF** needs a hardened nozzle (assumed).

**UV.** Plan to inspect and reprint the sunlit ASA parts after about 5 years (assumed). In a year outdoors, ASA lost at most 4 % tensile strength in flat samples (meas [45]). It also held up through UV, condensation and freeze–thaw testing (meas [46]).

## 5. Conflicts and how the design resolves them

| Conflict | Resolution |
|---|---|
| Winter insulation vs the summer charge limit (trip at +41.5 to +42.6 °C) | White ASA everywhere, a top-only roof cap with a 20 mm ventilated gap, and 10 mm foam (no thicker: thicker foam cuts the winter charging window from 44 % to 31 % and barely reduces the cold hours). The pack sits low, the battery end faces SE, and the box sits behind the panel. A SE side shade is added only if the summer log passes 38 °C |
| PMS7003 relative to the BME680 shield | Separate housings with no shared air. The PMS hood is under the box at the SE end with its exhaust to the SE. The shield is on the N side, below the box (warm air and exhaust rise away), about 0.3 m or more from the PMS outlet (calc, rough). The Cat5e gland is at the NW end, nearest the shield |
| ESP32 antenna keep-out vs the pack, metal, cables and PETG-CF | The antenna sits top-right inside a modelled 15 mm keep-out body. The pack is ≥ 60 mm away, the SMA about 100 mm, and all cables enter at the floor about 80 mm below. Nylon PCB screws; no insert within 20 mm; PETG-CF only ≥ 15 mm away. The metal mast about 45 mm behind is accepted and RSSI-tested (§ 7) |
| Sealing vs condensation | The box is sealed (cord and glands) but breathes through an ePTFE vent. A desiccant is swapped yearly and the PCB is coated. The PMS hood and the shield are open and drain freely |
| Wall thickness: sealing (2–3 mm) vs Wi-Fi detuning; B6 asked 3.0 mm, M5 2.4 mm | 2.4 mm solid walls everywhere. The 15 mm air gap makes the wall at the antenna uncritical. 2.4 mm also takes SMA bulkheads (≤ 2.8 mm) and IP67 USB-C parts (1–4 mm) without local thinning |
| Espressif's 15 mm vs the AISLER guide's 5–8 mm | Use Espressif's 15 mm. The cost is about 15 mm of box size |
| PETG-CF (stiff, on hand) vs sun, creep and RF | PETG-CF only for shaded parts that carry no seal and sit ≥ 15 mm from the antenna (PMS cradle, retainer, clips). The bracket is ASA, not PETG-CF as B15/B21 proposed, because it carries clamp load and its edges see sun (M3, M12) |
| Printed TPU gasket vs a bought cord | Use the EPDM cord. Printed gaskets leaked in tests (meas [13]). TPU 95A takes a 60–70 % compression set at 55–70 °C (ds [24]), and its Tg is about −24 °C (ds [25]). The TPU ring of B20 is only a tested fallback |
| Snap-fits vs cold | Screws into inserts everywhere |
| Strength vs sealing orientation | The box prints opening-up, so the land is an ironed top surface. Ears get 45° tapers. Tabs lie on the bed. Ribs run full depth (vertical in print). The arm prints flat with its length in XY |
| Cable < 1 m vs siting (shield away from the box, anemometer high, PMS ≥ 1.8 m) | Box floor at 1.90 m, PMS inlet at about 1.80 m, shield sensor at 1.60 m on the N side, Cat5e route about 0.85 m. The shield is closer to the box than S14's 0.5 m but below it and on the shaded side. Lengthening the Cat5e or the anemometer cable is decision 6.9 / 6.10 |
| Roof overhang (B12: 30 mm) vs a 256 mm bed | Side overhang 12 mm, lid side 25 mm |
| Shield arm (S14: 250 mm) vs a 256 mm bed | 160 mm in one straight print; about 220 mm diagonal is optional |
| Vent "under the roof overhang" (B8) vs a low side-wall position | Not possible: the roof is at the top. The vent goes low on the NW wall; brief snow cover is accepted (assumed harmless) |
| USB-C access vs leak paths and box width | No outside port. A vertical receptacle facing the lid; plug in with the lid off. An edge receptacle on the right edge would need about 25 mm more width, beyond the bed |
| Box behind the panel: summer shade vs winter sun | The battery end faces SE, so low winter sun still reaches it while the SW afternoon sun is blocked (assumed) |
| Conformal coating vs the BME680 | The breakout stays out of coating, or is masked with silicone-free tape; siloxane-free coating only (ds [82]) |
| Insects vs rime and airflow | No mesh on the shield louvres. Removable stainless mesh on the PMS inlet and outlet, cleaned when readings go wrong |
| Black plate undersides vs one filament | All white for v1. Black undersides only with an AMS |
| Fan aspiration vs the energy budget | No fan. Calm-sun errors are documented and flagged |
| PM in fog vs the power for a dryer | No dryer. Flag PM above 75 % RH |
| Disagreements between findings | Groove 4.0 wide, not 3.9 (B4). Insert hole 4.1, not 4.0 (M9 over B5). Cells vertical, not horizontal (B18 fails the geometry). Rod clearance 4.6, not 4.4 (M16 rule). Boss height 16.9, not 17.6 (cone geometry). PMS lead through a floor gland plus a hood notch, not a gland inside the hood (the floor under the battery is occupied). PMS hood screws at its ends, not 60 mm apart. Bed temperature 100 °C, inside every source range |

## 7. Gaps, and what to test on the first prints

**Inputs still needed from the owner**
1. Mast: outside diameter, material, and the height available for the box.
2. Outside diameter of each cable: Cat5e, solar, anemometer, PMS lead, vane.
3. BME680 breakout: board size, holes, power LED, regulator.
4. PMS7003: positions and sizes of the inlet and outlet; the adapter board size; the ribbon length; JST-PH or JST-XH.
5. SR10-36M panel: size and bracket.
6. 868 MHz antenna: model and SMA vs RP-SMA.
7. Exact ASA and PETG-CF brands, for HDT and shrinkage values.
8. Is a hardened nozzle fitted? Is there an AMS?
9. Typical maximum snow depth at the site.
10. Is the mast near a road that is salted in winter?

**Not sourced** (covered by conservative rules instead)
- RF loss or resistivity of PETG-CF at 868 MHz and 2.4 GHz.
- Solar absorptance of white ASA filament.
- Lapp's recommended gland hole size.
- The exact SMA bulkhead drawing.
- A sourced lid-screw spacing and a sourced sun-shade air gap.
- Rime clogging of printed shields.
- BME680 self-heating at one reading per 15 min.
- A mesh size proven against spiders.
- Gland sealing on printed layer lines.

**The thermal model** did not include this exact layout: box behind the panel, roof cap, battery end facing SE. Its numbers are for a generic white box.

**First prints and tests, in order**
1. **Bed fit.** Load the box (242 × 162), lid, roof (239 × 103), bracket (240 × 130) and arm into Bambu Studio with brims. Check each against the plate and any excluded area.
2. **Shrinkage.** Print the 150 mm L-bar in white ASA, measure after 24 h, and set "Shrinkage (XY)".
3. **Hole and insert coupon,** printed both flat and in a vertical wall:
   - M3 insert holes at 4.0 / 4.1 / 4.2;
   - M4 holes at 5.6 / 5.7 / 5.8;
   - gland holes at 12.2 / 12.3 / 12.4 and 16.3;
   - the SMA D-hole;
   - clearance holes at 3.4 / 4.6.

   Install the inserts at 260–265 °C and check them for pull-out and spin.
4. **Groove coupon.** A 60 mm straight groove with an R9 corner. Lay in the 3.0 mm cord and check 0.8 mm protrusion and about 80 % fill. Press a flat plate onto it and check that it closes on the hard stop.
5. **Wall coupon.** A 50 × 50 × 50 cup with the box settings. Fill it with water, or soapy water, overnight (ds [12]).
6. **Box hose and freeze test.**
   - Put dry tissue inside, close the lid to the stop and hang the box as mounted, with cable stubs in the glands.
   - Spray every face, the seam and every gland with a garden hose from about 3 m for 3 min.
   - Freeze at −20 °C overnight, thaw, and spray again.
   - Pass means the tissue stays dry. This is an IPX5-like check, not a certification (secondary [64]; meas [16][26]).
7. **RSSI test.**
   - Measure ESP32 RSSI at the gateway with the board in the printed box, and again in open air, with the box mounted on the real mast.
   - Confirm that Wi-Fi OTA works.
   - Optional: compare a lid or wall printed at 2.0 mm and at 2.4 mm (meas [2]).
8. **PMS hood.** Measure the ports, set the port-face plate, print, and run the smoke test (§ 2.5).
9. **Shield.**
   - Print one plate first and check it for warping, overhang quality and boss height.
   - Then print the stack and check the sensor offset against a reference thermometer at night with wind above 2 m/s (ds [93]).
   - After the first hard rime, check that the gaps stay open.
10. **First summer.** Log the pack temperature (DS18B20). Add the SE side shade if it passes 38 °C.
11. **First winter.** Log the pack vs the air, and the outer-to-inner cell gradient [P1 D13].
